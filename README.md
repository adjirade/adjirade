## 📌 About Me
- I am a Telecommunication Engineering graduate from Institut Teknologi Sumatera (ITERA) with a GPA of 3.46/4.00, holding 3 BNSP certifications in network engineering, cyber security, and web development.
- I gained hands-on experience in IoT system development, network infrastructure monitoring, and fiber optic installation through an internship at PT PLN ICON PLUS SBU West Java. During my internship, I independently developed a real-time IoT-based monitoring system for Point of Presence (POP) infrastructure using ESP32, Kivy (Python), Supabase, and OpenCV — covering temperature monitoring, smoke detection, automated cooling, and face recognition-based access control.
- I have a strong passion for network infrastructure, telecommunications, and cybersecurity, and I am committed to continuous learning and contributing to building reliable and efficient technology infrastructure.
